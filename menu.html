A working cafe menu — breakfasts served all day, jacket potatoes baked through, paninis pressed hot, salads on the side. Vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free options across the board.

A note on prices. Prices change with the seasons and the wholesalers — we're keeping them off the website so what you see in the cafe is always current. Pop in or call 01553 760057 if you'd like a quote for a group or a takeaway.
