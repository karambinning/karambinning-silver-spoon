about us

A clean room, hot food, and a proper cup of tea.

Silver Spoon is a family-run cafe and restaurant on Norfolk Street, a short walk from King's Lynn's old town. We open early, we cook everything fresh, and we treat the kitchen — and the rest of the room — like our own front porch. That's why people come back.

Whether it's the Builders Breakfast before a long shift, a jacket potato at lunch, or a quiet panini in the afternoon, you'll get the same thing: real food, generous helpings, no fuss.
