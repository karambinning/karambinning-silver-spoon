Silver Spoon is one of those places King's Lynn quietly loves — the cafe where the breakfast is hearty, the room is clean, and the welcome is real.

We open early, we cook fresh, and we look after every plate that leaves the kitchen.

How we do things.

Silver Spoon is a family-run cafe and restaurant on Norfolk Street, in the heart of King's Lynn. We've been quietly building a regular following over the years — through generous breakfasts, the kind of jacket potatoes that fill a lunchbreak properly, and a counter where the people behind it actually know their customers.

What we care about.

A clean room. That comes first — visitors mention it in every other review, and we take that seriously. Beyond that, hot food served hot, fresh ingredients, generous helpings, and prices that don't make you wince. We're not chasing trends. We're cooking good British cafe food the way it should be done.

Who we welcome.

Everyone. We've got wheelchair access, highchairs for the little ones, vegetarian and vegan options on the board, and gluten-free choices across the menu. Pop in, walk-ins are always fine — and if you've got a question about what's in something, just ask at the counter.
